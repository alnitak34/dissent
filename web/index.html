What just happened

On Jh Jd with board 5c 9s 2h 6h 4c, the old gate authorized a call, but the pressure-conditioned equity fell below its promised margin. The adapter returned violation 1 against the allowed maximum 0. Dissent credited the 3.00 test MON bounty plus the returned 0.10 test MON deposit. The earlier expired seal's 0.10 test MON deposit went to the agent. Both credits were withdrawn.

This tests a specific historical v1 rule, not all of Alnitak. The policy was developed after observing part of the history; prospective validation and external audit remain pending. The seven transactions cost 2.549064762375447460 test MON in gas; a 3.1 MON credit is not a 3.1 MON net profit.

Why did the rule go from 0 to 1?

Zero means the initial policy input contains no counterexample; it is not a probability or a claim that the agent is universally safe. For this historical hand, the exact old-model equity was 56.8942%, above the required 42.4170%, so the old gate would allow the call. Under the pressure model specified by this adapter, exact equity was 32.0046%: below that same required margin. The adapter therefore returns 1. These are model-based numbers, not proof of the opponent's actual cards. No LLM judges the submitted evidence.

Beyond this poker hand

The hand is the proof. The protocol is the product.

Other agents also produce numbers that drive actions: risk scores, prices, probabilities or spending limits. A rule that sounds safe may have an allowed case that breaks it. A log records the error; a bounty gives someone a reason to find it.

Dissent provides escrow, sealed challenges and settlement on Monad. Each field needs its own deterministic, bounded recomputer to define what counts as a valid counterexample. Risk and pricing examples are possible integrations, not integrations we have built or validated.

The economic gap

The proposed use is a funded bounty when finding a breaking case is hard, checking it is deterministic, and the underlying decision controls meaningful value. Demand from independent users is not yet established.

Trust boundary

The protocol guarantees the mechanism.
The adapter defines the meaning.

Dissent is not an oracle of truth. Production users should require immutable, audited and publicly verified recomputers. This testnet adapter has not undergone an external audit. The boundary is explicit in the protocol instead of hidden behind an AI evaluator.
