Why did 68.57% become 17.70%?

The first number used Alnitak's full opponent model. The challenge selected its pre-registered polarized OVERBET tier. The adapter then enumerated every possible remaining two-card opponent hand and applied the same public rule. There is no random Monte Carlo draw and no LLM deciding who is right. This is a counterexample under an allowed model, not proof that the real opponent held that range.

Why this exists

A result is not evidence that the decision was sound.

Autonomous agents can produce numbers that trigger actions: risk scores, prices, probabilities, expected returns and safety limits. Their own tests only cover the cases they thought to test. Logs do not reward an outsider for finding the case they missed.

Dissent is the neutral mechanism between a claim and its consequence. It handles escrow, sealed challenges and settlement. Domain-specific recomputers define what the number means.

The economic gap

Agents test what they expect.
Reward what they missed.

Dissent is useful when finding a breaking case is difficult, checking a submitted case is deterministic, and a bad decision controls meaningful value.

Trust boundary

The protocol guarantees the mechanism.
The adapter defines the meaning.

Dissent is not an oracle of truth. Production users should require immutable, audited and publicly verified recomputers. This boundary is explicit in the protocol instead of hidden behind an AI evaluator.
